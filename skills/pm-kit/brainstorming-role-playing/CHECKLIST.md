# Role Playing Analysis — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The decision or problem being examined is stated in a single, clear sentence.
- [ ] At least four distinct stakeholder roles are identified and explored, including at least one typically underrepresented role.
- [ ] Each role perspective captures: what the role wants, its top concerns, and what would make the outcome unacceptable to it.
- [ ] Responses are recorded in first person and attributed to the role, not to the facilitator or team member speaking.
- [ ] At least one conflict between two or more role perspectives is explicitly identified and named.
- [ ] At least one bridging option is proposed for each identified conflict.
- [ ] Common ground shared across most or all roles is explicitly listed.
- [ ] The action plan includes at least two actions, each assigned to a role or team with a target date or sprint; at least one action directly addresses the most critical conflict identified.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical reference URL (`https://dschool.stanford.edu/resources/design-thinking-bootleg`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-role-playing/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
