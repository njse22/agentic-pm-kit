# Brain Writing Round Robin Session — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The ideation prompt is stated as a single, focused sentence — specific enough that all participants respond to the same question.
- [ ] At least four participants (by role or name) are recorded, confirming silent, independent contribution before group review.
- [ ] All three rounds are complete: Round 1 (initial ideas), Round 2 (building on received ideas), and Round 3 (refinements); no participant slot is left blank.
- [ ] Ideas from all rounds are captured and consolidated; exact duplicates are removed and close variations are preserved.
- [ ] Ideas are clustered into named themes; each cluster has at least two ideas.
- [ ] The top three to five prioritized ideas are identified with a clear rationale for selection.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical reference URL (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-3-5_Brainwriting`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-brain-writing-round-robin/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
