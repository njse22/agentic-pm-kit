# Assumption Reversal — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The session topic is clearly identified and recorded.
- [ ] At least six assumptions are listed in the inventory — each phrased as a declarative statement, not a question.
- [ ] Each reversal is the direct logical inverse of its corresponding assumption (not a restatement, synonym, or weakening).
- [ ] Each consequence statement addresses what would change in the plan, design, or decision — it does not merely restate the reversal.
- [ ] Two or three reversals are marked as prioritized, with a written rationale explaining the selection.
- [ ] Each prioritized reversal has a concrete follow-up action, an assigned owner (role or name), and a target date or sprint.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Assumption Reversal reference URL (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateral_thinking`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-assumption-reversal/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
