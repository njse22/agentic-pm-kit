# Decision Tree Map — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The root decision is stated in one sentence and is a genuine decision point (the team has agency) rather than a risk event or an observation.
- [ ] At least two first-level branches are documented, each representing a distinct option.
- [ ] All paths terminate at a labeled outcome node — no branch is left open-ended or incomplete.
- [ ] Chance events are distinguished from decision points and outcomes in the node inventory.
- [ ] Probability or impact estimates are recorded where the user supplied them; no numbers are fabricated.
- [ ] At least two key paths are described with their sequence, probability assessment, and impact summary.
- [ ] A recommended path is stated with a rationale tied to the documented paths.
- [ ] The next action after the decision is named with an owner and a target date.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Decision Tree reference URL (`https://kepner-tregoe.com/training/decision-analysis-refresher/`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-decision-tree-mapping/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
