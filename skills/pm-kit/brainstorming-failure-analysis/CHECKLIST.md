# Failure Analysis — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The failure statement is concrete, bounded in time, and identifies a specific event — not a general pattern or ongoing condition.
- [ ] The timeline contains at least four events in chronological sequence, each capturing a change in project or process state.
- [ ] Contributing factors are identified across at least two of the three categories (Process, Communication, Resource).
- [ ] The root cause is a systemic condition or process gap — it does not attribute blame to a named individual or group of people.
- [ ] The impact assessment covers all four dimensions (Cost, Schedule, Quality, Scope); entries are marked "N/A" only if genuinely not applicable.
- [ ] At least two lessons are extracted, each stated as a durable, actionable principle — not a restatement of what went wrong.
- [ ] At least two corrective actions are defined, each linked to a lesson and targeting the root cause or a contributing factor — not a downstream symptom.
- [ ] Each corrective action is assigned to a role or team and has a target date or sprint.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Failure Analysis reference URL (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Failure_analysis`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-failure-analysis/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
