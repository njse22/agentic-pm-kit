# Six Thinking Hats — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The focus statement is specific — it names a concrete proposal or decision, not a general topic area.
- [ ] The White Hat section distinguishes verified facts from data gaps; no opinions are recorded as facts.
- [ ] The Red Hat section captures emotional reactions without justification requirements — both enthusiasm and unease are present if expressed.
- [ ] The Black Hat section lists substantive risks and concerns; it is not empty or dismissive.
- [ ] The Yellow Hat section lists genuine benefits; it is not empty or a simple rebuttal of Black Hat items.
- [ ] The Green Hat section proposes at least one alternative or improvement beyond the original proposal.
- [ ] The Blue Hat synthesis draws a clear conclusion supported by the preceding five hats and names at least one next step with an owner and target date.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Six Thinking Hats reference URL (`https://www.debonogroup.com/services/core-programs/six-thinking-hats/`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-six-thinking-hats/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
