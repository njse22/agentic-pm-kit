# Alien Anthropologist — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The subject observed is specific: a named workflow, process, interaction, or concept — not a broad domain.
- [ ] The raw observation section uses no insider jargon; every term is explained as for an uninitiated outside observer.
- [ ] The catalogue of the strange contains items that were listed without defense or explanation during collection.
- [ ] Every catalogued item is classified as Explained, Historical, or Unknown in the explanations table.
- [ ] Embedded assumptions are derived from Historical or Unknown items and include a validity assessment.
- [ ] The implications section distinguishes load-bearing assumptions from risk assumptions from redesign candidates.
- [ ] At least two concrete recommendations are produced, each with a named owner and a target date or milestone.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical field-study reference URL (`https://www.nngroup.com/articles/field-studies/`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-alien-anthropologist/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
