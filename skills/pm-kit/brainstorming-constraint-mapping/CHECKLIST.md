# Constraint Mapping — Acceptance Checklist

- [ ] The challenge scope is stated in a single sentence and represents the project challenge, goal, or decision being analyzed.
- [ ] At least five constraints are listed in the inventory, each phrased as a declarative statement.
- [ ] Every constraint is classified as Hard, Soft, or Imagined — not left blank.
- [ ] Every constraint has an impact rating (High / Medium / Low) — not left blank.
- [ ] All Soft and Imagined constraints rated Medium or High impact have a concrete action, an assigned owner, and a target date.
- [ ] Hard constraints are documented as-is with no action required — they are not erroneously assigned action items.
- [ ] At least two viable solution pathways are identified that respect only the confirmed Hard constraints.
- [ ] The output cites the canonical Constraint Mapping reference URL (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_constraints`).
- [ ] The "Source consulted" section records which mode was used (offline or online) and does not include fabricated quotations or page numbers.
- [ ] The facilitation dialogue is in the language at `language.communication`, and the saved artifact is in the language at `language.output` (falling back to `language.communication` if `language.output` is absent or empty).
- [ ] The artifact is saved to `docs/pm-kit/outputs/brainstorming-constraint-mapping/<slug>.md` and begins with the three-line provenance header.

> Mark **PASS** or **FAIL**. On **FAIL**, return to the skill with notes.
